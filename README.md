📌 About the Project

This project investigates the impact of thermal effects on resonant frequency in LC resonant inverters.

Resonant inverters are widely used in high-frequency power electronics because of their high efficiency and soft-switching capability. However, the components in the LC resonant tank are temperature-sensitive, which can cause changes in their electrical characteristics.

As the temperature of the components changes, the resonant frequency can shift from its designed value. This may result in:

- Resonant frequency deviation
- Loss of Zero Voltage Switching (ZVS)
- Increased switching losses
- Reduced efficiency
- Changes in inverter performance

The project analyzes these thermal effects and studies their impact on the performance of resonant inverters.

🎯 Objectives

- Study the temperature dependency of resonant components.
- Analyze thermal-induced resonant frequency shifts.
- Investigate the effect of frequency shift on Zero Voltage Switching (ZVS).
- Analyze the resulting impact on inverter efficiency.
- Verify the results using MATLAB and LTspice simulations.
- Develop a thermal resonance calculator for analyzing frequency variations.

💡 Project Outcome

The project demonstrates that temperature variations in the LC resonant tank components can cause a shift in the resonant frequency of the resonant inverter.

The study shows that this resonant frequency shift can affect the intended Zero Voltage Switching (ZVS) condition, potentially increasing switching losses and reducing converter efficiency.

The results were analyzed and verified through MATLAB and LTspice simulations, providing a better understanding of thermal effects in high-frequency resonant inverters.

The findings can help in developing more reliable, efficient, and thermally stable resonant power converters.
